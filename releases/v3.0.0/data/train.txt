DOOF Training Data Format
========================

Each line in this file is one training example (a response DOOF learned).
DOOF trains on this text to learn your communication style and knowledge.

Adding lines here is the simplest way to teach DOOF new things.
Lines can be any length. Short lines teach simple facts.
Longer lines teach conversational style.
